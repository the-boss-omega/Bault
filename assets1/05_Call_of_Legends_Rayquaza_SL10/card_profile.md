- For high-value purchases, use an established seller, protected payment method, return rights, and third-party authentication.

        ### Storage recommendations

        - Do not handle the card surface with bare fingers.
- For raw cards: use a clean inner sleeve, rigid holder, and archival storage box.
- For graded cards: use a slab sleeve and a fitted protective case.
- Store upright in a stable, dry environment away from direct sunlight and rapid temperature changes.
- Do not expose the card to cleaners, adhesive labels, PVC pages, or pressure from overfilled binders.
