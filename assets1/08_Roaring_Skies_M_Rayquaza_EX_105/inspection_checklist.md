# Inspection checklist — XY—Roaring Skies M Rayquaza-EX #105

        ## Identity
        - [ ] Card name, collector number, set symbol, language, and artwork all match.
        - [ ] Printed text, energy symbols, fonts, borders, and copyright line look authentic.
        - [ ] Card dimensions and stock feel consistent with a genuine Pokémon TCG card.

        ## Front
        - [ ] Centering is strong enough for the intended grade.
        - [ ] No scratches, dents, creases, pressure marks, stains, or residue.
        - [ ] Holofoil/texture behaves correctly under moving light.
        - [ ] No ink touch-up, recoloring, or surface restoration.

        ## Back
        - [ ] No whitening, chips, scratches, dents, stains, or color loss.
        - [ ] Corners are sharp and not compressed or reshaped.
        - [ ] Blue border and Poké Ball print are correctly registered.

        ## Edges and structure
        - [ ] No trimming, sanding, peeling, splitting, rebacking, or layer separation.
        - [ ] No waviness or unusual gloss suggesting alteration.

        ## Card-specific risks
        - Texture authenticity
- Front and back centering
- Edge chipping
- Corner whitening
- Surface scratching or dents
- Correct Mega Evolution text and Δ Evolution mark

        ## Graded-card checks
        - [ ] Certification number verified directly with PSA.
        - [ ] Online slab image, label data, and physical slab match.
        - [ ] Slab has no evidence of tampering, frosting, cracks, resealing, or label substitution.
        - [ ] Seller ownership and current timestamp are verified for remote purchases.

        ## Transaction safeguards
        - [ ] Recent completed sales for the same exact grade were reviewed.
        - [ ] Seller history and return policy were checked.
        - [ ] Protected payment, insurance, tracking, and signature confirmation are used.
        - [ ] Independent authentication or escrow is used when the price justifies it.

        Target profile: PSA 10 GEM MINT.
        This checklist does not assign a grade or authenticate a card.
